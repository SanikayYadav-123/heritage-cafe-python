02 / OUR BEGINNING

Started with coffee.
Stayed for the people.

In 1987, Heritage started as a tiny neighbourhood coffee counter with six seats and one very good espresso machine.

Over the years, the room grew, the menu evolved and the team became a family. What never changed was our belief that hospitality should feel personal.

Today, we still bake every morning, brew every cup with care and know that the best part of a café is the people who fill it.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Less rush.
More ritual.

We believe good food doesn't need to be complicated. It needs good ingredients, honest preparation and enough time to do things properly.
